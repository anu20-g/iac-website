DATA GOVERNANCE & AUDITING PRIVACY POLICY

Regulatory Compliance: Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, India & Inter-Enterprise Security Mandate
Operating Standard: Zero Commercial Telemetry / Strictly Administrative Retention
Revision Cycle: Continuous Legal Verification

1. Zero Commercial Tracking & Public Telemetry Ban

Intellectual Asset Corporation does not employ tracking cookies, advertising analytics, behavioral profiling pixels, or consumer demographic aggregators. This digital interface operates in passive broadcast mode. No client-side storage, local caching of identifier tokens, or third-party analytical beacons are injected into visitor runtimes.

2. Corporate Credentials & Intake Communications

All communications routed to IAC institutional gateways and dispatch registries are subjected to mandatory counterparty screening. By transmitting formal inquiries or documentation, the transmitting party consents to:

(a) Verification of corporate incorporation records, jurisdictional registration numbers, and signatory authorization.
(b) Forensic audit logging of transmitted dispatch metadata, routing headers, and document hashes.
(c) Permanent retention of submission dossiers within IAC's secure compliance archives for anti-infringement surveillance.

3. Enterprise Node Telemetry & License Auditing

Where an enterprise sub-license is executed, IAC deploys cryptographically signed licensing validation keys. Such nodes transmit minimal runtime state verifications solely to validate cryptographic integrity, core count restrictions, and geographic field-of-use compliance. Node telemetry never intercepts client data payloads or proprietary licensee datasets.

4. Non-Disclosure & Information Walls

IAC enforces a strict informational wall. Counterparty licensing records, audit reports, and proprietary documentation are never commercialized, shared, or brokered to external marketing aggregators. Disclosure is restricted strictly to:

(a) TRDL core engineering and legal officers under reciprocal non-disclosure protections.
(b) Statutory regulatory bodies or Indian arbitral tribunals operating under lawful judicial subpoena.
