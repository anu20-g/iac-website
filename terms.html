1. Scope of Authority & Inter-Entity Relationship

TRDL Intellectual Asset Corporation (TRDL-IAC), domiciled in the Republic of India, operates strictly as the exclusive commercial sub-licensing, distribution, and contractual auditing vehicle for Tewary Research and Development Lab (TRDL). TRDL-IAC does not hold, claim, or represent ownership of foundational patents, physical theory repositories, model weights, or underlying source code. Any representation by third parties to the contrary is fraudulent and void ab initio.

2. Closed-Capital Firewall & Solicitation Ban

TRDL-IAC is structured as a closed-capital operating vehicle. TRDL-IAC does not seek, solicit, entertain, or accept equity investment, convertible notes, venture debt, or syndication capital. All unsolicited equity or investment communications will be discarded without acknowledgment and may be archived for legal intelligence and anti-encroachment records.

3. Stringent Field-of-Use & Defensive Restrictions

All sub-licenses authorized by TRDL-IAC are strictly non-transferable, revocable upon breach, and confined to contractually negotiated operational boundaries. Licensees are expressly barred from:

(a) Reverse-engineering, decomposing, or reconstructing pre-geometric formulations, Information Disturbance Model (IDM) equations, or Geodesic Intelligence Operating System (GIOS) runtime logic.
(b) Integrating TRDL assets into automated kinetic weaponry, non-consensual surveillance architectures, or offensive military apparatus.
(c) Re-distributing, sub-licensing, or transferring deployed runtime artifacts without prior written ministerial consent from TRDL-IAC Directorate.

4. Mandatory Derivative Grant-Back Covenant

Every sub-license granted by TRDL-IAC incorporates an unalterable reciprocal grant-back condition. Any algorithmic optimizations, runtime tuning heuristics, architectural mutations, or hardware-specific acceleration matrices derived from TRDL frameworks immediately and automatically become the intellectual property of TRDL, licensed back to TRDL under perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free covenants.

5. Sovereign Digital & Runtime Audit Verification

TRDL-IAC reserves the unilateral, non-negotiable right to conduct unannounced digital inspections and architectural telemetry verifications of all enterprise instances running TRDL-licensed assets. Refusal of audit access constitutes an immediate material breach, triggering automated node revocation and liquidated damages.

These Terms, together with any executed sub-licensing schedules, are governed exclusively by the laws of the Republic of India. Any controversy, claim, or breach shall be resolved via mandatory binding arbitration conducted in New Delhi, India, pursuant to the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996. The proceedings shall be conducted in English by a single arbitrator appointed by TRDL-IAC Directorate.
