PROCUREMENT & COMPLIANCE PROTOCOL

IAC does not engage in open-market software sales. Access to TRDL frameworks requires a formal auditing process.

01. Initial Ingestion
Formal transmission of corporate incorporation filings, jurisdiction credentials, and target framework petition through authorized institutional corporate dispatch. Direct sales solicitations are discarded without processing.
02. Compliance Audit
Automated routing of the mandatory 12-point Legal Intake Questionnaire (Form CA-01). The audit requires full beneficial ownership disclosure, explicit technical deployment bounds, and absolute anti-reverse engineering architectural commitments.
03. Executive Execution
The compliance dossier must be formally executed and certified by the applicant organization's Chief Technology Officer (CTO) or Chief Legal Officer (CLO). Submissions lacking C-suite or legal signatory authority are rejected ab initio.
04. Continuous Auditing
Execution of non-transferable, heavily restricted sub-license incorporating mandatory TRDL reciprocal derivative grant-backs, quarterly royalty audits, and unannounced bi-annual operational inspections.
