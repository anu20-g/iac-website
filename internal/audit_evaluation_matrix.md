## 1. Statutory Evaluation Matrix

Every incoming Form CA-FORM-01 (Commercial Sub-License Application & Compliance Audit) must be adjudicated against this five-pillar matrix. Any single Red Flag trigger constitutes a mandatory, immediate rejection without exception.

| Audit Category | Approval Criteria (Proceed to Stage 2) | Immediate Rejection Trigger (Red Flag - Disqualify) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Corporate Ownership** | Transparent capitalization table; clear identification of beneficial owners maintaining &ge; 25% equity. Unconditional acceptance of change of control termination. | Submission via anonymous shell entities, opaque offshore trusts, or SPVs; refusal to acknowledge the Change of Control termination clause. |
| **Deployment Scope** | Narrow, highly specific commercial enterprise integration (e.g., "B2B financial data parsing runtime"). | Vague operational scope; attempts to deploy in restricted sectors (defense, kinetic systems, foundation AI training, pre-geometric physics R&D). |
| **Derivative Works** | Unconditional acceptance of the perpetual, royalty-free reciprocal grant-back provision for all architectural optimizations. | Demands intellectual property retention over modified core frameworks; attempts to strike, redline, or dilute the grant-back clause. |
| **Access & Security** | Source code and runtime access strictly siloed to a verified, internal engineering or DevOps team with isolated subnet access. | Plans to outsource deployment to third-party vendors, external contractors, offshore development agencies, or shared cloud tenants. |
| **Audit Adherence** | Full consent to bi-annual, unannounced technical, architectural, and financial reviews conducted unilaterally by IAC. | Attempts to restrict IAC audit authority, require advance notice exceeding 24 hours, or refuse access to backend deployment logs and telemetry nodes. |

## 2. Operational Adjudication Rules

### Rule 1: The Zero-Negotiation Policy
If an applicant triggers a Red Flag regarding beneficial ownership transparency or the reciprocal grant-back provision, the file is closed immediately. Under no circumstances may an IAC representative attempt to negotiate compromised terms that dilute the TRDL Master License Agreement.

### Rule 2: Verification of Technical Controls
If an applicant clears initial screening and asserts compliance with anti-reverse engineering controls, require submission of a secondary technical exhibit:
- **Exhibit B-1:** A mandatory, one-page network architecture schematic demonstrating cryptographic key isolation and verifiable sandboxing of TRDL runtime binaries from general enterprise networks.

### Rule 3: Rejection Communication Protocol
When an applicant is disqualified, compliance officers must never provide specific legal justifications, detailed audit scores, or debate points. Issue exclusively the standardized rejection communication.
